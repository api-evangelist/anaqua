By Rohit Saluja , Head of AI, Anaqua As organizations race to adopt AI, many IP teams are finding themselves managing a growing collection of disconnected tools, copilots, point solutions and custom applications. Each may deliver value on its own. Together, they scatter the context that AI depends on, and create new problems around governance, data quality, security, user adoption and decision-making.
