Every patent and trademark ultimately hinges on a calendar. Miss a critical deadline and an invaluable asset can vanish with no chance of revival. Docketing – the unglamorous work of tracking, organizing and managing the strict legal deadlines, correspondence and milestones behind intellectual property (IP) assets – is the backbone of every IP practice.
